Hi, I’m Sanvi Kaushik 👋

I study Software Engineering at Carleton University . Over the past few years, I’ve worked across some very different corners of tech, from data engineering at Scotiabank , to 5G and RAG systems at Ericsson , to connected-vehicle software at BlackBerry . I’ve realized I just really like understanding how the machine works, whatever the machine happens to be.

Lately, I’ve been especially drawn to medtech and problems that stop feeling abstract the moment they affect someone you care about. I love building under pressure, chasing problems that do not want to be solved, and taking on things that make me a little nervous before I start.

Outside of tech, I love Bollywood dancing. I even got to perform at Parliament when I was younger. I’m also a big hiker, and Angel’s Landing in Zion National Park is still my favourite hike. If you ever want to talk through a good idea, throw a hard problem my way, or just grab a coffee, say hi.
